About Formula 1

The pinnacle of motorsport — where engineering brilliance meets raw human courage at the edge of 230 mph.

🏎️

The Cars

Hybrid power units producing over 1,000 HP, with turbochargers spinning at 175,000 RPM. Zero to 60 mph in under 2.5 seconds.

🌍

Global Sport

Racing across 5 continents in 2026 — from the historic streets of Monaco to the blazing desert of Saudi Arabia and the legends of Suzuka.

⚙️

Precision Engineering

Over 10,000 parts per car. Carbon fiber chassis, active aerodynamics, and energy recovery systems pushing the boundaries of science.
